Output-tail redaction closes four known shapes, and a new setting hides the tail (phase 348; rec-20261116-001, rec-20261117-002). When `build-test-must-pass` refuses or is bypassed on a failing `verification.testCommand`, it prints the run's output tail on stderr after redaction. Four named shapes that got through are now redacted: a credential flag (`--auth-token value`, `--db-pass=value`) or `-u user:password` at the very start of the captured text; a credential-named environment value that starts with `/` and contains `+` or `=`, which was taken for a path; a quoted `Authorization`, `Proxy-Authorization`, `Cookie` or `Set-Cookie` key (any letter case) with a quoted value, such as `"Authorization":"Bearer …"`; and a run of two or more consecutive words of a multi-word secret value, when the run is at least 8 characters and the value has at most 12 words. Redaction of the output tail is best-effort: only the named shapes are covered, and a secret printed in any other shape still reaches stderr. For a consumer that captures settle's stderr and cannot accept that, the new optional config key `verification.buildTestOutputTail` takes `"show"` or `"hide"`; absent means `"show"`, and `cadence init` does not write it. The tail is shown unless the setting is `"hide"`. Under `"hide"` the gate prints `output tail: (hidden by config; N lines captured)` in place of the tail on every refusal and on the bypass notice, and the `SUMMARY.json.gateBypasses` entry's `detail` says the lines were captured and hidden by config. The tail is never recorded under either value, and with the key absent the gate's stderr, flags and `detail` are unchanged.
