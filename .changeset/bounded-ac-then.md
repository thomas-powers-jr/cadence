Bounded ACs and the literal AC pass (phase 350; rec-20261121-005). `cadence draft check` now prints an `UNBOUNDED_AC_THEN` warning for each AC whose Then-text uses one of the universal words `any`, `every`, `each`, `same` or `unchanged`, matched case-insensitively as whole words (a word is a run of ASCII letters), up to the first line that begins with `Not covered:` after leading spaces and tabs. The message names the words and asks for named example inputs and outputs or a `Not covered:` line. It is a warning only: a warning-only result exits 0, and `draft approve` still approves such a DRAFT, showing the message as a `coherence-warn` anomaly (a stderr line with the default notify transport) when `anomaly-notify` is in the cell. The `bugfix` and `refactor` DRAFT templates now state their preserved-behavior ACs without a universal word, so `draft new --template bugfix`, `--template feature` and `--template refactor` produce DRAFTs with no `UNBOUNDED_AC_THEN` warning; their AC headings are as before. The parser's Then capture is still unanchored, so a capitalised `Then` followed by a space or line break anywhere above an AC's Then line (heading, Given or When) starts the scanned text early (rec-20261124-001).
