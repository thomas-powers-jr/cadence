# 312-01 — pack declares phase-build as a required skill

## Objective

`cadence/core-skills` declares `skillAudit.required: ["phase-build"]`, making the pack a real behavioral contributor rather than a commands-only declaration, and producing the first non-empty `skillAudit.provenance` in the corpus — `[]` in all 322 settle records to date. Phase 294 omitted the field only because the `Skill`-tool matcher was missing and telemetry never populated; phase 295 fixed that, so the blocker is gone. Two measured constraints bound what this phase may claim. First, per `rec-20260917-006`, `state.skillAudit.invoked` is deduped, append-only and never reset, so the requirement proves a skill appears in a checkout's invocation history rather than that this phase invoked it — the live pass path therefore cannot demonstrate enforcement, and a fixture is the only place the refusal path can be proven. Second, the requirement is not phase-conditional (phase 294 D-AW), and `phase-build` appears in only 4 of the 16 phases 295-310, so `--allow-skill-audit-miss` is expected to be routine rather than exceptional; phase 311 made that bypass record in `SUMMARY.gateBypasses`, which is why this work was sequenced after it. Source: `rec-20260917-007`.

## Boundaries

- DO NOT add `gates[]` to the manifest, or any pack payload beyond `skillAudit.required`.
- DO NOT build phase-conditional required skills. That mechanism does not exist and is out of scope; the non-conditional constraint is phase 294's D-AW.
- DO NOT modify `runSkillAuditCheck`, `checks/pack-resolution.ts`, or the phase-311 `gateBypasses` push. This phase declares a requirement; it does not change how requirements are enforced or recorded.
- DO NOT modify `PackManifestZ` or any Zod schema — `skillAudit.required` is already an accepted optional field.
- DO NOT attempt to fix the checkout-scoped `invoked` semantics (`rec-20260917-006`). That is a mechanism change and belongs to its own phase.
- DO NOT claim in any doc, comment, changeset, or SUMMARY that the pack enforces that every phase ran through `phase-build`. It does not; it requires the skill to appear in the checkout's invocation history.
- DO NOT loosen, skip, or delete an existing test to accommodate the new requirement.
- DO NOT edit historical phase artifacts, `.cadence/ROADMAP.md`, or `MILESTONES.md`.
