## Objective

Land the substance of Dependabot #474 on a fresh branch: bump the root devDependency `@changesets/cli` from `^2.31.1` to `^3.0.3`, delete the two `pnpm.overrides` keys it leaves dead (`read-yaml-file@1.1.0` and `js-yaml@4.2.0`, which `scripts/check-lockfile-overrides.mjs` reports as `unresolved-target` on #474), and regenerate `pnpm-lock.yaml`. Changesets 3.x drops `braces` from the tree, so this phase also retires the operator-approved `GHSA-vfj7-8cjw-p6xm` exception before it expires on 2026-11-18. That leaves the exceptions table empty, the healthy state per the phase 260/297 precedent. Pre-draft probe (2026-10-13, in this worktree): after the bump and key removal, `pnpm why braces -r` and `pnpm why js-yaml -r` are both empty, the overrides checker passes with 7/7 targets satisfied, and `pnpm audit` reports only the 13 known root moderates (`rec-20261008-002`, out of scope). A throwaway `changeset version` run under 3.0.3 bumped all five fixed-group packages to 1.70.1 in lockstep and left testkit untouched, and `extractChangelogEntry` parsed the new `## 1.70.1` heading. The one output difference is cosmetic: fixed-group packages with no changesets of their own now get a `No changes in this release.` line under their heading where 2.x left it empty. No published package changes, so there is no changeset.
