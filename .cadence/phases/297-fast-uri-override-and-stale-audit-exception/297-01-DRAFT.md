# 297-01 — Pin fast-uri past the advisories and retire the resolved hono exception

## Objective

CI is red on every PR to `main`, for two independent reasons, neither of them
introduced by any PR. The `Security` workflow was already failing on `main` at
`d8d19ad4`.

**The `audit` job fails on four unlisted high advisories.** All four are the same
package: `GHSA-5jgf-p345-68v8`, `GHSA-f65p-4m7j-42xc`, `GHSA-fph4-wmhf-6fwf` and
`GHSA-jqff-g426-hqxp`, every one against `fast-uri`, every one patched in
`>=3.1.6`, every one reached through `packages/core > @modelcontextprotocol/sdk`.
There is already a `pnpm.overrides` entry for this package — `"fast-uri@3.1.2":
"^3.1.5"` — and it is wrong twice over. It under-shot the fix by one patch
version, so the lockfile resolves `fast-uri@3.1.5` while the advisories require
`>=3.1.6`; and having done that bump, its key `fast-uri@3.1.2` now matches
nothing in the tree, making it the stale silent-no-op override key that phase
253 wrote `scripts/check-lockfile-overrides.mjs` to catch.

**The `test` job fails on one expired doc row.** `docs/security/audit-exceptions.md`
documents a single exception, `GHSA-88fw-hqm2-52qc` for `hono`, with expiry
`2026-08-28`. Its own justification says *"Re-check on the next
`@modelcontextprotocol/sdk` bump"*. That bump has happened — `package.json` asks
`^1.29.0` and the tree resolves `1.30.0` — and the advisory no longer appears in
`pnpm audit` at all. The row is stale as well as expired and should be removed as
resolved, exactly as phase 260 removed three vitest/vite/postcss rows once they
were fixed outright.

Removing it empties the table, which trips a third thing:
`security-ci.test.ts:213` asserts `rows.length > 0`. That assertion encodes phase
182's snapshot — when the table genuinely did document live advisories — as
though it were an invariant. It is not. **Zero documented exceptions is the
healthy state**, and a gate that forbids it pressures a future maintainer to keep
a dead row alive to stay green. Drop the count requirement, keep every
well-formedness and non-expiry check for whatever rows do exist.

Filed as `rec-20260907-004` on the phase-296 branch.

## Boundaries

- DO NOT extend any exception's expiry date. An expiry is the gate; pushing it forward defeats the gate and is the one thing this phase exists to avoid.
- DO NOT address the two moderate advisories. The `audit` job fails on high/critical only, so they are out of scope and would enlarge the diff for no CI benefit.
- DO NOT modify `scripts/check-audit-exceptions.mjs` or `scripts/check-lockfile-overrides.mjs`. If a gap is found in either, record it as a recommendation instead.
- DO NOT weaken the per-row well-formedness or non-expiry assertions while removing the row-count requirement.
- DO NOT touch `packages/core/src/verify/host-cli-client.ts` or anything else belonging to phase 296; this branch is cut from `main` and must merge cleanly ahead of PR #481.
